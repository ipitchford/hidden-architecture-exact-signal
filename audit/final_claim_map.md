## Novelty adjudication

The independent literature audit records 108 queries, including a 14-query rerun after the all-q construction. Its result is clear within the recorded corpus for the three principal theorem units. This is bounded negative search evidence, not a proof of historical priority. The manuscript uses 'we prove', with no 'first' claim.

Known ingredients are attributed: stationary-flux merging, log-sum contraction, phase-type/transfer cancellation, hidden-nonequilibrium detection, and symmetric-divergence inequalities. The precise earliest affinity–Jeffreys attribution remains unresolved, so the paper supplies its elementary proof and makes no separate novelty claim for it. Sason2015 is cited as related divergence literature, not as an assertion that it contains the exact affinity-constrained lemma.

Owen, Kolchinsky and Wolpert2019 were identified during independent review. Their hidden-state thermodynamic advantage concerns implementing a conditional distribution under potentially time-dependent dynamics. The final introduction explicitly distinguishes it from the fixed complete stationary path-law problem and cites it.

## Rejected inferences

- A finite numerical scan is not a certificate of V∞ or V4.
- p(S)1=0 is sufficient for the constructed models but not necessary for arbitrary hidden representations.
- Exact transfer degree two does not imply two hidden phases suffice thermodynamically.
- Strict lower bounds for individual finite models do not imply a strict inequality after the unrestricted infimum.
- Nonattainment with fixed r,m,f0 does not imply nonattainment with fixed entire observed law.
- The all-q constructive saving may be very small and is not claimed to be optimal or experimentally detectable.
- Internal AI review does not constitute external journal peer review, publication acceptance, or an official REF decision.

## Verification separation

The central exact certificate independently computes the entire transfer numerator/denominator by polynomial determinants and Cramer's rule, then encloses entropy by rational series. Separate checks use the ODE annihilator, direct resolvents and flux formulas. High-precision finite cases test the all-q construction; the written derivative/remainder proof establishes its universal quantifier. The underlying optimiser traces are included as discovery records, including failures, and do not support a global-minimum claim.

All paper claims are restricted to finite irreducible stationary time-homogeneous CTMCs, deterministic binary labels and even microscopic variables under ordinary reversal. Natural logarithms and kB=1 are fixed throughout.
